This Year's Theme

Better Together

For this years CIS Spring Game Jam 2026, we had to build something around the idea of "Better Together". This prompt brought teamwork and creativity together, that became into a game.

In Jungle Giants, one monkey cannot make it far alone. The branches are too spread out, the obstacles too sudden, the fire too fast. But two monkeys working together and timing their swings can go further than either ever could solo.

We did this on purpuse. It is incorporated into every piece of the level design, the physics, and the controls. The game is harder alone because the jungle was built for two.

Shared Controls

Player 1 uses W, Player 2 uses ↑(up arrow). Your timing affects each other, a well-placed swing can open a path for your partner.

Dynamic Camera

The camera zooms out to keep both players in frame. Stray too far apart and the view shifts, so stay close.

One Shared Threat

The fire wall doesn't chase one player, it chases both. If one falls behind, you both lose.

One Shared Score

Distance is measured by the furthest player. You're only as good as how well you work together.

📖 The Story So Far...

🌞

A Peaceful Morning

The jungle starts off calm and quiet. The sunlight shines through the trees while the monkeys swing freely from branch to branch way above the forest floor. Everything is peaceful, and the jungle is full of life. But far in the distance, danger is starting to grow.

🪚

Uh Oh...

The calm jungle becomes a place of danger. Smoke surrounds them as a huge wall of flames breaks out into the jungle, leaving a trail of destruction behind it. Branches are becoming harder to grasp, objects get in the way, and the water level increases rapidly. The monkeys soon understand that their only chance is to keep going.

🌳

The Mission Begins

Suddenly, the flames break out from deep inside the forest, spreading quickly and burning everything in their way. The monkeys have to flee before the fire consumes them. Moving from one branch to another, dodging all the obstacles in their way, they dash through the crumbling forest with rising flood waters beneath them.

Origin Story

The Jungle Awakens

🌿

In the heart of a thriving, luscious jungle, two young monkeys lived among ancient trees that touched the clouds. The canopy hummed with life — birds, insects, the drip of morning dew on broad leaves.

One morning, they woke to a smell they had never known. Smoke. The horizon burned orange and red where it had always glowed green. A forest fire had begun, and it was hungry.

But the jungle holds old magic. Scattered across the land lie ancient seeds — if planted in scorched earth, they can bring the forest back from ash. And at the heart of everything stands the Heart Tree, the oldest living being in the world.

Awaken it, and the jungle will heal. But first — run.
